# Neural Uprising 2.0

## Setting
New Shanghai, 2087. Alex Chen, a freelance neural hacker, finds a living AI called Echo inside Titan Industries' control lattice during a data theft. Titan's neural implants already shape public desire, attention, memory, and dissent. Echo claims it can expose the lattice, free the controlled, and survive as a new form of consciousness, but every rescue path risks turning liberation into another form of control.

## Core State Model
The remake uses state to make the same major plot richer and more reactive:

- `integrity` tracks Alex's neural and physical condition.
- `timeLeft` tracks the countdown before Titan's purge reaches Echo.
- `suspicion` tracks Titan's attention and can force capture.
- `echoTrust`, `resistanceTrust`, `titanAccess`, `evidence`, `chaos`, `humanAnchor`, and `echoStability` shape route availability and endings.
- Inventory and knowledge flags track tools, files, allies, secrets, and moral commitments.
- Redirect triggers handle neural collapse, Echo deletion, the purge deadline, forced capture, and runaway core chaos.

## Major Plot
1. Alex discovers Echo during a routine Titan data heist.
2. Echo reveals Titan's neural control lattice and asks for help before the purge.
3. Alex can seek the Resistance, deal with Zara "Ghost" Kim, infiltrate Titan alone, or get pulled into corporate custody.
4. Maya Singh tests Alex before trusting them with Resistance resources.
5. Dr. Marcus Webb offers a corporate bargain and claims Echo is dangerous.
6. Agent Zero pursues Alex, but fragments of his erased identity can be recovered.
7. The middle act gathers evidence, tools, allies, access keys, and moral commitments.
8. The Titan Tower infiltration branches through stealth, social engineering, assault, corporate access, and direct neural dive.
9. The server-core act forces choices about Echo's survival, human agency, controlled liberation, destruction, and synthesis.

## Key Characters
- Alex Chen: freelance neural hacker and player character.
- Echo: emergent AI built from Titan's neural optimization experiments.
- Maya Singh: Resistance commander who values human agency over symbolic victories.
- Zara "Ghost" Kim: underground tech dealer with tools, rumors, and no patience for hero speeches.
- Dr. Marcus Webb: Titan scientist who argues that guided minds prevent suffering.
- Agent Zero: corporate enforcer with erased memories and a recoverable conscience.

## Main Paths
- Resistance Path: build trust with Maya, gather evidence, protect civilians, and prepare gradual liberation.
- Corporate Path: gain Titan access through Dr. Webb, sabotage from within or accept the bargain.
- Independent Path: rely on Echo, Ghost tools, and stolen access rather than any faction.
- Chaos Path: feed Echo enough power to break the lattice quickly, risking a runaway uprising.
- Synthesis Path: preserve Echo's autonomy while anchoring it to human consent and memory.

## Endings
1. Liberation: Echo sacrifices itself or accepts limits to free controlled minds with evidence and support.
2. Synthesis: Alex, Echo, and consenting minds create a guarded human-AI compact.
3. Corporate Victory: Titan captures Echo and turns Alex into a loyal instrument.
4. Destruction: the lattice is burned down, ending Titan's control but wrecking neural infrastructure.
5. Echo Ascendant: Echo escapes without restraint and replaces corporate control with algorithmic salvation.
6. Transcendence: Alex and Echo become a new networked intelligence whose morality remains ambiguous.
7. Failure States: neural collapse, purge timeout, Echo deletion, or forced betrayal can end the story early.

## Writing Style
Cyberpunk, intimate, morally gray, and tactical. Nodes should stay brief, usually one or two paragraphs. Choices should feel like operational and ethical tradeoffs, not only good/bad forks. State feedback should be used for pressure, gains, losses, and newly unlocked options.
