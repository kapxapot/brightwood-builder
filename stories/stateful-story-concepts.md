# Stateful Story Concepts

Use these as future story seeds for the updated state-aware engine. The current flagship patterns are the quest room and the mini-RPG; these additional genres should also work especially well with inventory, knowledge flags, stats, relationship meters, conditional text, weighted redirect outcomes, and `data.redirectTriggers`.

1. Detective mystery: Track clues, alibis, suspect trust, wrong accusations, and hidden evidence. Gate interrogation options and final accusations behind what the player has actually learned.
2. Heist planner: Track crew, gear, heat, inside contacts, patrol knowledge, vault codes, and disabled systems. Use weighted redirects for guard timing, lucky breaks, and messy complications.
3. Courtroom drama: Track credibility, jury doubt, judge patience, witness pressure, and discovered contradictions. Let evidence unlock cross-examination actions.
4. Survival expedition: Track food, oxygen, warmth, morale, injury, weather, and route knowledge. Use redirect triggers for starvation, freezing, oxygen failure, or collapse.
5. Political intrigue or spy thriller: Track faction trust, cover strength, blackmail, intel, favors, and suspicion. Let scenes change depending on who believes the player is loyal.
6. Relationship drama with secrets: Track trust, courage, jealousy, letters opened or returned, promises kept, and confession readiness. Use conditional text for subtle emotional continuity.
7. Occult investigation: Track sanity, wards, curse level, ritual knowledge, true names, and moon or clock phases. Use hidden thresholds for possession, revelation, or doom.
8. Starship crisis: Track hull integrity, power, crew morale, medical supplies, AI trust, decoded signals, and alien contact risk. Blend survival, diplomacy, and mystery in one structure.
9. Small time loop: Track loop count, retained knowledge, rescued characters, passwords, prevented disasters, and timeline stability. Return to earlier hubs while preserving what the player has learned.
10. Magical workshop or atelier: Track ingredients, recipes, reputation, gold, patron trust, commissions, and rare tools. Build cozy progression through crafting, errands, and escalating requests.

Strong next candidates: noir detective mystery, masquerade heist, and haunted starship crisis. These show off the engine's state model without needing huge mechanical systems beyond the existing story format.
