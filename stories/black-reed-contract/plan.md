# The Black Reed Contract - Story Plan

## Setting

Brindleford is a poor border village built where peat fields, old graves, and drowned alder woods meet. The black reeds around it sing at night, cattle are found hollowed by water, and villagers blame a marsh monster they call the Reedwife.

The player is Ivo Thorn, a gray-mantled contract hunter hired to end the killings. The contract looks simple at first: find the monster, learn its habits, and kill it. The deeper truth is older and uglier. Brindleford once survived by keeping a pact with the fen, and the current mayor has broken that pact for profit, hiding a child's stolen name at the center of the curse.

## RPG Structure

- Target length: 106 nodes.
- Tone: dark folkloric adventure with monster-hunting investigation, bitter humor, rural suspicion, curses, and morally gray choices.
- Runtime state: health, coin, rations, clues, trust, dread, mercy, weapons, wards, tools, companion, investigation flags, pact evidence, and lair state.
- Main regions: Brindleford, the drowned mill, oath-barrow, widow's cottage, black chapel, mayor's hall, deep fen, island camp, old dam, eel shrine, Reedwife's lair.
- Node balance: action hubs for investigation choices, skip nodes for consequences, redirect nodes for chance and danger, redirect triggers for collapse states, and six finish nodes.

## Key Beats

1. Arrive in Brindleford under a suspicious contract and decide how to take the job.
2. Choose a starting kit: moon-iron blade, witch-salt satchel, or oath-lantern.
3. Choose one companion: Ylva the marsh guide, Brother Orek the bell-priest, or Marn the gravedigger.
4. Prepare in the village by buying a reed map, buying rations, praying, or reading old records.
5. Investigate the drowned mill, oath-barrow, and widow's cottage to gather clues about the Reedwife and the missing child.
6. Enter the black chapel, recover bell evidence or silver ash, and decide whether to confront Mayor Wex.
7. Cross the deep fen through boat, map, or hard wading while managing health, dread, and supplies.
8. Camp on a reed island, survive a night omen, and choose final preparations at the old dam or eel shrine.
9. Enter the lair, decide whether to parley, trap, or attack, then choose the fate of the Reedwife and Brindleford.

## Possible Endings

- Broken Hunter: health reaches zero before the contract is resolved.
- Fen Taken: dread overwhelms the hunter before the lair is reached.
- Contract Paid: the Reedwife is slain and Brindleford survives, but the old pact is gone.
- Pact Restored: the mayor's crime is exposed, the stolen name is returned, and the fen accepts a harsher peace.
- Child Freed: the curse is separated from the child's spirit, leaving both village and fen wounded but alive.
- Bitter Mercy: the hunter walks away or yields judgment to the fen, leaving Brindleford to pay what it owes.

## Branching Notes

Keep the investigation broad but shallow: the mill, barrow, and widow branches should return to the reed-edge hub. The chapel and mayor scenes narrow the story toward the fen. Final choices should be gated by collected clues and mercy, but the player should always have a path to finish the contract.
