# The Ember Crown - Story Plan

## Setting

Asterfall is a mountain kingdom whose old royal magic has gone wrong. The Ember Crown, once used to seal winter spirits below the citadel, has been stolen by Lord Malrec. Villages are freezing in summer, roads are full of raiders, and old places have started waking up.

The player is a young oath-taker sent from Elderglen to recover enough ember shards and royal signs to open the Sky Citadel, confront Malrec, and decide what should happen to the crown.

## RPG Structure

- Target length: exactly 180 nodes.
- Tone: fantasy quest with light tactical choices, exploration, companions, treasure, and several fights.
- Runtime state: HP, gold, supplies, rope, torch, map, shield, weapon, companion flags, blessings, sigils, ember shards, and final-knowledge flags.
- Main regions: Elderglen, Wildwood, Ruined Keep, Sunken Marsh, Ashen Mine, Sky Citadel, Ember Throne.
- Node balance: action hubs for choices, skip nodes for consequences, redirect nodes for chance and combat outcomes, and a small set of finish nodes.

## Key Beats

1. Choose a path: blade, scout, or rune.
2. Choose a companion: Liora the healer, Bram the guard, or Knox the lockpick.
3. Prepare in Elderglen with optional shopping and shrine blessings.
4. Explore the Wildwood, face wolves, meet the Thorn Court, and seek the first ember signs.
5. Push through the Ruined Keep, dealing with bandits, ravens, chapels, towers, and old royal marks.
6. Cross the Sunken Marsh, where will-o'-wisps, lizardfolk, cages, and a drowned shrine test the party.
7. Enter the Ashen Mine, fight kobolds and an ogre, and collect deeper ember power.
8. Climb the Sky Citadel, pass mirrors and guardians, and reach Malrec's throne.
9. Choose the fate of the Ember Crown.

## Possible Endings

- Death on the road: HP or a lethal combat result ends the quest.
- Crown broken: the crown is destroyed and Asterfall must rebuild without royal magic.
- Crown claimed: the player becomes ruler, carrying both power and danger.
- Mercy at the throne: Malrec is spared or transformed, and the crown's curse is gentled.
- Shadow bargain: the player serves the crown's darker will.

## Branching Notes

Keep the story narrow enough to remain playable. Most branches should return to the current region's main route within a few nodes. Use loops for town preparation and limited exploration, but carry the quest forward after each area. Avoid creating more than the planned finish nodes.
