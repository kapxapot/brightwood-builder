# Celtic Shadows: A Dark Fantasy Tale

## Setting
Ancient Ireland, where the veil between the mortal world and the Otherworld (Tír na nÓg) has grown dangerously thin. Dark forces from Celtic mythology threaten to break through, led by the Fomorians - monstrous beings of chaos and darkness.

## Main Character
A young druid's apprentice who discovers they have inherited ancient blood ties to both the mortal world and the Sidhe (fairy folk).

## Key Story Points
1. Discovery of ancient powers and heritage
2. Encounters with mythological beings (both helpful and hostile)
3. Journey through cursed forests and ancient burial mounds
4. Confrontation with dark forces from Irish mythology
5. Choices between power and wisdom, vengeance and mercy
6. Understanding the balance between light and shadow

## Story Branches
1. Druidic Path
   - Focus on wisdom and natural magic
   - Alliance with benevolent Sidhe
   - Protection of sacred sites

2. Warrior Path
   - Learning combat from Celtic warriors
   - Using ancient weapons
   - Direct confrontation with dark forces

3. Shadow Path
   - Embracing darker powers
   - Deals with dangerous entities
   - Walking the line between good and evil

4. Diplomatic Path
   - Negotiating with various supernatural factions
   - Building alliances
   - Finding peaceful solutions where possible

## Possible Endings
1. Guardian of Balance (Good)
   - Becoming a protector of both worlds
   - Establishing lasting peace between realms

2. Warrior Champion (Good)
   - Defeating the dark forces through strength and courage
   - Becoming a legendary hero

3. Shadow Keeper (Neutral)
   - Maintaining balance through understanding of both light and dark
   - Walking a solitary path between worlds

4. Corrupted Guardian (Dark)
   - Falling to the temptation of dark power
   - Becoming what you sought to defeat

5. Sacrifice (Bittersweet)
   - Giving up your own existence to seal the breach between worlds

## Key Elements to Include
- Celtic deities and mythological creatures
- Sacred sites and ancient rituals
- Moral choices with significant consequences
- Elements of Irish folklore and traditions
- Dark fantasy atmosphere with moments of hope
- Personal character development through choices
