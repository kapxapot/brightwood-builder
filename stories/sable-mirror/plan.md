# 🕵️ The Sable Mirror

## Setting

Sable Harbor, 1937: rain, neon, river fog, and a city police department too tired to distinguish justice from convenience. During the opening gala of the Glasswater Hotel, stage illusionist Lys Rowe is found dead behind a locked mirror wall in the penthouse observatory.

The player is Detective Mara Vale, called in because the room looks impossible and because the victim left a final message on a blackened mirror: "THE CITY SAW."

## Core Mechanics

- Track time left before the prosecutor closes the file.
- Track focus, heat, pressure, public trust, leverage, clue count, evidence score, and wrong accusations.
- Track five suspects: Clara Voss, Silas Hale, Mira Quill, Orson Bell, and Ilya Rook.
- Track trust meters and alibis for suspects.
- Track physical clues, hidden evidence, contradictions, access routes, motive threads, and final proof.
- Use conditional actions to unlock deeper interrogation and final accusation paths only after discovery.
- Use redirect nodes for chance-based searches, witness reliability, tailing scenes, and risky confrontations.
- Use redirect triggers for running out of time, losing focus, and letting department heat boil over.

## Suspects

- Clara Voss, hotel singer and Lys's former lover. She knows the hotel mirrors and is hiding a private farewell.
- Silas Hale, city council fixer. He has debt, access, and political reach.
- Mira Quill, crime columnist. She collected secrets from everyone and loved one secret too much.
- Orson Bell, hotel engineer. He built the mirror wall and sold duplicate keys.
- Ilya Rook, river courier. He moved packages through service corridors and knows who crossed the roof.

## Major Threads

1. The locked mirror room is not locked if someone knows the service reflection corridor.
2. The poison was carried in stage cosmetic, not a glass of champagne.
3. Lys was blackmailing Hale and Bell with a ledger page.
4. Clara lied about timing to hide a goodbye, not a murder.
5. Mira's column draft names the killer but omits the delivery route.
6. Ilya delivered a false rose box and saw a second key.
7. Orson's maintenance ledger proves who could enter the mirror wall.
8. Hale arranged the murder, but Bell enabled the access and Mira nearly exposed it.

## Endings

- Case Goes Cold: time runs out before a strong accusation.
- Badge Surrendered: heat overwhelms the department and the case is buried.
- Burnout: focus collapses under pressure.
- Wrong Accusation: the player points to the wrong suspect or lacks proof.
- Narrow Conviction: Hale is charged with enough evidence, but some rot survives.
- Perfect Case: Hale falls, Bell confesses access, Clara is cleared, and the city cannot bury the mirror ledger.
