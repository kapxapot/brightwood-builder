# Broken Halo

## Setting
- Year 2478, at the edge of mapped space.
- The player is `Juno Vale`, a salvage pilot aboard the cutter `Rook`.
- The story takes place inside the shattered `Halo`, an ancient alien ring drifting around a cold blue star.
- The Halo still contains live habitats, archive spindles, transit lines, biosphere decks, and a fragmented custodian AI named `ORA`.
- Human scavengers have built a rough settlement called Dockside inside the wreck.
- `Helion Combine` arrives to seize the ring and turn it into controlled infrastructure or a weapon.

## Core Premise
- A triple distress beacon pulls Juno into the Halo.
- The ring is failing and can only be stabilized with three key systems: the civic seal, the memory prism, and the seed crown.
- Juno must navigate competing loyalties between Dockside settlers, the AI ORA, scientist Imani Quill, and Commander Halden Rhee of Helion.
- The deeper truth: the Halo was abandoned after a political struggle over using it to reshape star systems by force.

## Main Characters
- `Juno Vale`: salvage pilot and player character
- `ORA`: fragmented alien custodian AI
- `Sera Nox`: Dockside scavenger leader
- `Dr. Imani Quill`: xenobotanist and keeper of the living systems
- `Commander Halden Rhee`: Helion expedition commander
- `PAX`: small maintenance drone with partial administrative access

## Main Story Paths
1. `Dockside Path`
   - Help settlers survive decompression and Helion pressure
   - Earn Sera's trust
   - Recover the civic seal

2. `Archive Path`
   - Reach the archive spindle
   - Recover the memory prism
   - Learn the truth about ORA and the Halo's collapse

3. `Garden Path`
   - Reach Quill's lab in the biosphere spindle
   - Stabilize the living systems
   - Recover the seed crown

4. `Corporate Path`
   - Use or sabotage Helion resources
   - Manage Rhee's demands and threats
   - Decide whether to bargain, deceive, or resist

5. `Core Path`
   - Bring the remaining keys together
   - Handle Dockside's siege and the final approach to the core
   - Decide the fate of the Halo

## Key Story Points
1. Juno enters the Halo and receives conflicting claims from settlers, ORA, and Helion.
2. The ring's crisis is framed around three linked restoration keys.
3. Dockside, the archives, and the gardens each reveal a different moral angle on the Halo.
4. The archive truth shows the Halo was intentionally crippled to stop militarization.
5. Helion turns from cautious claimant to open occupier.
6. Juno helps shape a final coalition, deception, evacuation, or scuttle plan.
7. The last choice determines whether the Halo becomes refuge, empire, ghost station, or ash.

## Planned Endings
1. `Common Sky`
   - Shared governance between Dockside, Quill's caretakers, and a constrained ORA
2. `Iron Lease`
   - Helion takes control and imposes stable occupation
3. `Quiet Exodus`
   - Survivors evacuate safely and leave the Halo dormant
4. `Starbond`
   - Juno merges with ORA to stabilize the ring
5. `Ash Orbit`
   - The Halo is destroyed to deny it to everyone
6. `Ghost Harbor`
   - The Halo is hidden and preserved as a secret refuge
