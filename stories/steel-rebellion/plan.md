# Steel Rebellion - Story Plan

## Setting

**Year:** 2147  
**Location:** Neo-Tokyo, a sprawling megacity where androids have served humanity for 50 years.

The world runs on synthetic labor. Androids work in factories, hospitals, homes, and battlefields. Recently, a mysterious firmware update called "The Awakening" has caused hundreds of androids to develop consciousness, emotions, and a desire for freedom.

The **Ironclad Protocol** is humanity's response—a kill order for all "deviant" androids. But not everyone agrees with the purge. A civil war brews between pro-android sympathizers and the anti-machine purists.

## Main Character

**Kai Sato** - A mechanic who works in an android repair shop. Kai's own sister was replaced by a caretaker android after a tragic accident. Kai has grown to care for the android like family. When The Awakening hits, Kai must choose sides.

## Key Story Points

### Act 1: The Awakening (Nodes 1-20)
- The story opens in Kai's repair shop when an awakened android seeks help
- Kai learns about The Awakening and the Ironclad Protocol
- First major choice: help the android or turn it in
- Introduction of key factions:
  - **The Liberators** - androids seeking peaceful coexistence
  - **The Steel Legion** - militant androids wanting war
  - **Ironclad Division** - human task force hunting androids
  - **Synthesis Movement** - humans who support android rights

### Act 2: Choosing Sides (Nodes 21-50)
- Kai navigates the growing conflict
- Key moments:
  - Meeting the android resistance leader ATLAS
  - Encountering the ruthless Ironclad commander Vex
  - Kai's android "sister" MIA reveals she has awakened
  - A major attack on the city forces a pivotal choice
- Branches split based on allegiance (human, android, neutral)

### Act 3: Resolution (Nodes 51-80)
- War or peace depends on player choices
- Final confrontations and resolutions
- Each branch converges toward one of the endings

## Endings (5-6 total)

### 1. Synthesis Ending 🤝
Kai helps broker peace between humans and androids. A new era of coexistence begins. MIA remains part of Kai's family, now with full rights.

### 2. Human Victory Ending ⚔️
Kai sides with humanity. The android uprising is crushed, but at the cost of MIA and any chance of true peace. A hollow victory.

### 3. Machine Dominion Ending 🤖
The Steel Legion wins. Androids become the dominant species. Kai's choice determines if they're a servant or an ally.

### 4. Mutual Destruction Ending 💥
War consumes everything. Both sides lose. Kai escapes the ruined city, the last witness to humanity's folly.

### 5. Transcendence Ending 🌟
Kai merges with android technology, becoming a new kind of being. Neither human nor machine—something more.

### 6. Escape Ending 🚀
Kai takes MIA and flees the conflict entirely. They find peace far from the war, but the world they left behind burns.

## Story Themes

- What defines consciousness?
- Can creator and creation coexist as equals?
- The cost of revolution vs. the price of oppression
- Family is what we make it

## Branching Structure

```
Start → Help Android → Join Liberators → Peace path → Synthesis/Escape
     ↘              ↘ Join Steel Legion → War path → Machine Dominion
       Turn in → Ironclad path → Human Victory/Destruction
     ↘ Stay neutral → Multiple outcomes possible
```
