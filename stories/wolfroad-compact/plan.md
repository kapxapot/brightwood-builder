# The Wolfroad Compact

## Setting
The marchland of Harrowmere is caught between river towns, old hill shrines, border keeps, and proud noble houses after King Maelor dies on campaign. His last compact would place the young heir under a council of rivals rather than one ambitious regent, but the parchment must reach Highmere before the houses choose war.

## Core Tone
- Grounded political dark fantasy, not horror
- Muddy roads, wary halls, hard bargains, old oaths, and restrained low magic
- Moral pressure without hopelessness
- Adventure-forward pacing with travel, supplies, wounds, favors, secrets, and faction choices

## Main Characters
- You: Ren Arlen, a sworn courier and minor sword-bearer with the dead king's last compact
- Lady Maerla Veyne: the king's stern chancellor, trying to keep the realm intact
- Lord Garran Brant: a northern warlord whose banners can either defend the heir or dominate the council
- Lady Ilyra Brant: Garran's daughter, sharper than her father's captains and less eager for war
- Mira Fen: a river smuggler who knows every tollhouse and hidden ford
- Brother Caldus: keeper of Hollow Abbey and its unsettling records of royal oaths
- Captain Dain Locke: border commander holding starving soldiers together at Red Ford
- Princess Elowen: the young heir, present in the final council as more than a prize

## Key Story Points
1. Receive the compact and choose how Ren accepts the mission.
2. Travel through the Wolfroad, river market, Hollow Abbey, Red Ford, and hidden marsh paths.
3. Gather proof that raids and forged orders are pushing Harrowmere toward civil war.
4. Use inventory items such as the writ, seal ring, old map, river pass, iron key, black ledger, guest bread, herbs, and oath tokens to open hidden paths.
5. Let random road events feel physical and political: patrols, fog, broken carts, messengers, ambushes, supper insults, and council seating.
6. Reach Highmere and decide whether to restore the compact, back a regency, reveal the ledger, or leave the great houses to face the truth without Ren.

## Main Branches
1. Wolfhall branch: northern banners, hidden galleries, household rivalries, and the black ledger.
2. River branch: toll roads, Mira Fen, market unrest, smuggler routes, and a condemned squire.
3. Abbey branch: royal records, old hill oaths, crypt routes, and subtle prophecy.
4. Border branch: refugees, false raids, Dain's soldiers, and proof of manufactured war.
5. Marsh branch: hidden causeways, hermit bargains, and fast access to Highmere.

## Possible Endings
1. The Compact Holds: a difficult council peace gives the heir real protection.
2. The Armed Regency: order is preserved by soldiers and banners, with a price.
3. The Ledger Court: public truth breaks the conspiracy but leaves power unstable.
4. The Free Road: Ren refuses to become another court instrument and preserves the compact elsewhere.
5. Broken Road: wounds and hunger end the mission before Highmere.
6. Hunted Courier: suspicion closes every gate and the compact is seized.

## Writing Notes
- Keep public description in-world only.
- Keep paragraphs short and vivid.
- Keep action labels short and concrete.
- Use hidden conditioned actions where items or reputation matter.
- Use random redirects for road and council uncertainty.
- Aim for roughly 160 nodes with six total finish nodes.
